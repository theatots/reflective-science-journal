Submit Your Paper

We operate classic peer review, but your PDF might come out looking like a scrapbook. Here is how to join the chaos responsibly.

How review works

  • Double-anonymous. Reviewers never see your name until decision time.
  • No gatekeeping. We care about clarity, rigor, and originality; not institutional prestige.
  • Margin commentary. Every accepted paper comes back with design notes and optional zine-style layout edits.
  • Open methods. We strongly encourage sharing code, data, and lab notebooks.

This site is a concept demo journal. Use it as inspiration for real-world earth science projects, lab zines, or class assignments.

About Burn Book Earth Science

Burn Book Earth Science is what happens when you mix rigorous earth science with the energy of a 2000s teen movie. We believe scientists deserve spaces that are rigorous and playful, where your field notes can match your favorite album cover and your methods section reads like a director's cut.

Use this mock journal as a prompt: for class projects, lab retreats, or zine-making nights where everyone rewrites their most boring earth science paper in full Y2K glory.
